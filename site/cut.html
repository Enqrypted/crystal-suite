Video editing

Cut

A video editor laid out like Sony Vegas Pro: media, preview and properties on top, the timeline below. Cut, layer, mix and render to MP4 or WebM.

What’s inside

Edit like it’s Vegas

A real timeline

Video and audio tracks, split with S, trim, ripple editing, snapping to edges and markers, loop a time selection.

Vegas-style handles

Drag an event’s top corners for fades and its top edge for volume or opacity. Overlap two events for a crossfade.

Mix the audio

Event and track volume and pan, mute and solo, normalize, and a master bus with peak meters.

Picture tools

Transitions, Pan/Crop with Ken Burns moves, brightness, contrast, color, blur and more.

Titles and generators

Text, titles, lower thirds, solid colors and gradients.

Render As

MP4 (H.264 + AAC) or WebM (VP9 + Opus), up to 4K, with hardware encoding when your PC has it.

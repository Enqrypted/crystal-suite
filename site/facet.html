FAQ

Facet: FAQ

Does Facet move my desktop files?

No. Facet’s folders hold shortcuts, so nothing on your desktop is moved or deleted.

Does Facet slow down my PC?

No. Facet starts when you sign in and idles at well under 1% CPU.

Does the Spotify widget need my account?

No. It reads Windows’ own media controls, so it works with the Spotify app without signing in.

FAQ

Adobe alternative: FAQ

Is there a free alternative to Adobe Creative Cloud?

Yes. Crystal Suite is a free, open-source set of Windows apps for the everyday Creative Cloud jobs: Photon (like Photoshop), Quartz (like Acrobat), Cut (like Premiere Pro) and Opal (photo viewing), with more apps planned.

Can I open my Adobe files?

Photon opens and saves layered Photoshop PSD files, and Quartz opens, edits and saves PDFs. Other Adobe formats (AI, INDD, PRPROJ) aren’t supported.

Why is it free?

Crystal Suite is an independent open-source project under the MIT license. It has no ads, no telemetry and no paid version.

Is Crystal Suite as powerful as Adobe’s apps?

No. Adobe’s apps have many more features. Crystal Suite covers the everyday work—photo editing, PDFs, video—without a subscription.

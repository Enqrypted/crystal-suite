Photon

A layered image editor laid out like Photoshop, with its tools, panels and shortcuts. Retouch photos, build composites, and open or save PSD files.

What’s inside

A photo editor you already know

Layers that behave

Pixel, type, shape, fill and adjustment layers, groups, masks, clipping masks, and layer styles from Drop Shadow to Bevel & Emboss.

Real selections

Magic Wand, Quick Selection, lassos, Color Range, Select Subject, Select and Mask, and Quick Mask.

Adjustments and filters

Curves, Levels, Hue/Saturation and more as adjustment layers. Camera Raw, Liquify, blurs and distortions with a live preview.

A proper brush engine

Size, hardness, flow, pen pressure, jitter and smoothing, plus healing, patch, clone, pattern stamp and history brushes.

PSD in and out

Open PSDs with their layers and masks, save back to PSD, or keep everything editable in a Photon project.

Export Layer As

Export each layer trimmed to its content as PNG, JPEG or WebP, with a live preview of the result.

FAQ

Prism: FAQ

Does Prism block ads?

Yes. Prism blocks ads and trackers by default with the EasyList, EasyPrivacy and uBlock Origin filter lists, and hides leftover ad slots. A shield in the toolbar shows what was blocked and turns protection off for a site.

Is Prism based on Chromium?

Yes. Prism uses the same engine as Chrome and Edge, so websites work as you expect.

Does Prism collect data?

No. Prism has no account, no sync and no telemetry, blocks third-party cookies and upgrades sites to HTTPS.

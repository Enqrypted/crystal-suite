Why people look for a Photoshop alternative

Photoshop is the standard photo editor, but it is only sold as a subscription, needs an Adobe account and is a download of several gigabytes. If you edit photos now and then, retouch, make thumbnails or build composites, you pay every month for tools you use a few times.

A good free alternative should do the same everyday jobs, open the PSD files you already have, and not make you learn everything again. That is what Photon is built for: it copies Photoshop’s interface, so the Layers panel, the toolbar, the options bar and the shortcuts are where your hands expect them.

FAQ

Free Photoshop alternative: FAQ

What is the best free Photoshop alternative for Windows?

It depends on how you work. If you already know Photoshop, Photon is the closest match: it copies Photoshop’s layout, tools and shortcuts, opens and saves PSD files, and runs offline on Windows. It is free and open source, with no account.

Is Photon really free, or is it a trial?

Really free. Photon is open source under the MIT license: no trial, no watermark, no subscription and no paid version.

Can Photon open my PSD files?

Yes, with their layers, groups, masks, blend modes, opacity and clipping masks. Photon also saves layered PSD files that open in Photoshop.

Does Photon have Photoshop’s keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. V for Move, B for Brush, Ctrl+T for Free Transform, Ctrl+J for Layer via Copy and the rest of the usual shortcuts work as you expect.

What can Photoshop do that Photon can’t?

Photoshop has generative AI features, 3D, CMYK printing workflows, smart objects, actions and plug-ins, and runs on macOS. Photon focuses on the everyday photo editing, retouching and compositing most people use Photoshop for.

Does Photon work without the internet?

Yes. Everything runs on your PC; nothing is uploaded.

# Third-party software in Cut

Cut itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). It bundles:

- **Mediabunny** — Mozilla Public License 2.0, © Vanilagy. Source: https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny. Used unmodified for reading media files (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, WAV, Ogg, FLAC…), frame-accurate decoding through WebCodecs, and writing MP4/WebM files.
- **Electron / Chromium** — MIT and BSD-style licences, provided by the Crystal Suite launcher. Video/audio encoding and decoding use the codecs built into Chromium and Windows.
